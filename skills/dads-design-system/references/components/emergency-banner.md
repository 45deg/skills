# Emergency Banner

Purpose: Surface urgent alerts that must dominate the first view.

Use for immediate safety or similarly critical notices.

Avoid routine, long-lived, or low-urgency announcements.

Key rules:
- Use urgent error-like semantics.
- Never make it user-dismissible.
- Keep title explicit and urgent.
- Keep body short.
- Use at most one primary link.

Accessibility: Maintain high contrast and a simple unmistakable layout.

Related: `notification-banner`.
